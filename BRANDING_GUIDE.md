# Hive Branding Guide

## Brand Overview

**App Name**: Hive

**Brand Purpose**: A task management application designed to help teams and individuals organize work efficiently

**Brand Values**: Clarity, collaboration, productivity

---

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors

#### Yellow

- **Primary**: `#FFD700` (Gold) or `#FFEB3B` (Brighter)
- **Use for**: CTAs, highlights, active states, accents

#### Black

- **Primary**: `#1A1A1A` or `#212121`
- **Use for**: Text, backgrounds, structure, depth

### Secondary/Accent

#### White

- **Color**: `#FFFFFF` or `#F5F5F5` (off-white for backgrounds)
- **Use for**: Highlights, empty states, contrast, card backgrounds

### Supporting Palette

- **Light gray**: `#F0F0F0` (backgrounds, borders)
- **Medium gray**: `#757575` (secondary text, disabled states)
- **Dark gray**: `#424242` (hover states, dividers)
- **Error**: `#FF5252` (error states with black text)

---

## Color Usage Guidelines

### Applications

- **Primary CTA buttons**: Yellow background, black text
- **Text**: Black on white/light gray backgrounds
- **Active navigation**: Yellow highlight or underline
- **Focus states**: Yellow border with shadow
- **Loading/progress indicators**: Yellow
- **Success messages**: Can use yellow or green accent
- **Error states**: Red (`#FF5252`) with black text

---

## Typography

### Headlines

- Style: Bold, modern sans-serif
- Examples: Inter, Poppins, Montserrat

### Body Text

- Style: Clean, readable sans-serif
- Examples: Inter, Open Sans, Roboto

### Font Scale

- 12px, 14px, 16px, 18px, 20px, 24px, 32px

---

## UI Components

### Buttons

- **Primary**: Yellow background, black text, rounded corners
- **Secondary**: White/transparent background, black border/text
- **Tertiary**: Ghost style with hover effect

### Badges/Tags

- **Task Priority**:
  - High: Red
  - Medium: Yellow
  - Low: Gray
- **Status**:
  - In Progress: Yellow
  - Complete: Green

### Icons

- **Style**: Outline or filled solid
- **Color**: Black (can invert on yellow backgrounds)
- **Consistency**: Maintain uniform stroke weight

---

## Accessibility

- Ensure yellow/black contrast meets WCAG AA standards (7:1 ratio for text)
- Don't rely on color alone to communicate information
- Test with color-blind friendly palettes
